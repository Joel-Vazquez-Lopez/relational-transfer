# Conditional confirmation plan for step-3000 signal

Written while the original five-world run was still underway. Nine of its
fifteen pairs were complete. The step-3000 query-separation sign matched the
final held-margin sign in all nine; the locked step-1000 predictor had failed
to show an association. Thus this is a new, post hoc hypothesis that requires
an independent test.

Only run the confirmation if the original fifteen pairs pass the familiar
gate and at least thirteen of fifteen step-3000 signs match the final margin
sign. Report the original analysis regardless of whether this gate passes.

The independent test uses the first three qualifying 4-regular ten-node graph
isomorphism classes after generator seed 8: seeds 9, 14, 15. The focal pairs
are, respectively, (0,5) versus (0,8), (1,2) versus (1,5), and (0,1)
versus (0,4), with one versus two common neighbors. These were selected by
the same graph-only rule used for the first five worlds, before any model was
trained on them. Use new paired training seeds 10501 and 10502, 6000 steps,
and the unchanged model, matched episodes, final 1024-scene test, and
step-3000 query-separation measure.

Primary confirmation report: the six paired step-3000 separation differences
and final held-margin differences, their sign agreement, Spearman rank
association, and the three world-mean directions. Five or six sign matches
with all three world means aligned would support carrying the predictor to a
larger study. Fewer than five matches or a reversed world mean would count
against it. A positive result here would remain limited to this synthetic
role-transfer task; it would not establish a mechanism or an LLM result.

As a diagnostic, compare the step-3000 separation to the step-1000 signal,
step-3000 training loss, and familiar accuracy. This independent test does
not retroactively make the original step-3000 observation confirmatory.
