## 6. Query displacement/compression

Condition A compresses substitutions within UA: its per-seed `C(UB)-C(UA)` gaps are 1.197712, 1.167879, 1.183017, 1.155239, 1.185313, 1.189256, mean 1.179735986. This robustly replicates the D4-style final-query compression phenomenon.

Under the exact identity permutation in Condition B, the compressed role moves to UB. The fixed-identity `C_B(UB)-C_B(UA)` gaps are -1.165758, -1.152099, -1.159101, -1.183693, -1.174238, -1.156548, mean -1.165239472. Their negative sign is the opposite of compression remaining attached to the original identities and therefore fails the frozen fixed-identity alternative, as D5-A predicts.

All 60 normalized oracle-query vectors, raw vectors, per-substitution displacement rows, support strata, and cross-context consistency rows are preserved per run.

## 7. Candidate-redirection behavior

The behavioral interaction is uniformly positive across seeds (mean 0.656218998), exceeds the 0.20 raw-effect threshold, has bootstrap lower bound 0.653563161, and survives Holm correction at 0.031250. Thus the relocation is not only a representational-distance effect: changing a single endpoint query redirects the unchanged candidate set much more successfully for trained than withheld endpoint distinctions, and this advantage swaps under A/B role relocation.

Every underlying redirection row—including original/alternative meaning, pairwise score margin, pairwise ordering, and final redirected choice—is in each run's `redirection_focal_A.csv` and `redirection_focal_B.csv`.

## 11. Relationship to D4

D4 established a descriptive selective compression in the original frozen D1 computation: within-hole endpoint substitutions barely moved the final query and corresponding endpoint alternatives were not reliably reordered. D5 first replicates the relevant A-condition compression robustly across six fresh models (mean role gap 1.179735986; 6/6 positive), then relocates the training-support hole by an exact label permutation. Both query compression and candidate-redirection asymmetry move with that graph role. This converts the specific D4 descriptive association into causal evidence about the effect of the manipulated training-support topology in the D5 system.

D5 does not revise D4's separate conclusion that the old checkpoint lacked clean, context-independent constituent translations.

## 12. Observation, inference, and speculation

### Observation

Both A/B relocation interactions are positive in 6/6 seeds, exceed their frozen minimum effects, have paired-bootstrap lower bounds above zero, and survive their two-test Holm family. Familiar learning and all controls pass. Condition D's behavioral connectivity statistic passes but its geometry statistic does not. Condition C is approximately symmetric across UA and UB.

### Inference

Within this architecture, renderer, oracle channel, budget, and candidate construction, the exactly matched intervention licenses a causal statement: relocating which endpoint relations are absent from training relocates learned final-query compression and endpoint-redirection behavior. Identity labels alone do not explain the original compression, because the effect swaps under the A/B identity permutation. The degree-balanced C pattern weakens a simple globally privileged-identity explanation. The D result does not support the stronger claim that adding a two-edge path geometrically generalizes to its still-unseen closure.

### Speculation

A plausible hypothesis is that optimization allocates discriminative query geometry preferentially to endpoint contrasts demanded by the training support. Whether analogous allocation occurs in larger transformers, natural language, or LLM representations is unknown. D5 supplies neither linguistic evidence nor an LLM result.

## 13. What D5 establishes

- A technically valid, fully preregistered 24-model D5-A result.
- A large, seed-consistent causal effect of A/B training-support role on final oracle-query geometry.
- A matching causal relocation in exact candidate-redirection behavior.
- Robust replication of the original A-condition compression across six seeds.
- Evidence against compression being fixed to the original identity labels.
- No support for the stronger, separately corrected D connectivity claim because its geometry component fails.

## 14. What D5 does not establish

D5 does not identify layers, heads, neurons, registers, symbolic variables, or mechanisms; no activation patching or component search was performed. It does not establish compositionality in general, grammatical structure, linguistic universals, or any property of LLMs. It does not show that every support-graph manipulation produces the same effect, nor that the failed D connectivity geometry would emerge with more training or different hyperparameters. No such rescue run was conducted.
