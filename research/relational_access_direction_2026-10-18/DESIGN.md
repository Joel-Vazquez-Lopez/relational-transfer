# From developmental geometry to relational access

18 October 2026. Design proposal for review; not a frozen protocol or an executed experiment. No new model calls. The completed Atlas pilot and its failed competence gates remain unchanged.

Consequently, the general causal-developmental programme below is a synthesis of prior aims, not a newly established frontier. The outstanding requirement is a specified, identifiable test that predicts fresh selective intervention outcomes across models beyond the surviving surface/output rivals. A proposed complete developmental graph has not been recovered or validated. Any later experiment requires an explicit account of what it adds to these completed or stopped studies. The format diagnostic below is a bounded diagnostic proposal, not evidence that this larger requirement has been met.

## Question

When does a linguistic relationship become usable for different questions, and does its causal influence generalize across wording, grammatical realization and answer format?

This retains the original interest in development across layers and tokens. The proposed comparison is between patterns of functional influence, measured separately within each model. Similar hidden-state curves alone are insufficient.

## What motivates it, and what does not

The repaired Atlas pilot passed its numerical checks. Qwen answered all 24 participant questions correctly but answered No to all eight mention-order questions. Llama answered Yes to all eight Goal questions. Neither model passed a complete development-family competence screen; no held-out model outcomes were observed.

These are different questions about different facts. Correct participant answers do not establish that mention order was represented correctly, and constant binary answers do not establish a readout bottleneck. Nonzero hidden-state interactions also occurred on incorrect answers.

## First proposed experiment: diagnose the same fact across formats

Start with Qwen and the already exposed development passages. Use them for calibration only. Do not consume the original unexecuted held-out families while revising questions.

Example context: Ada recommended Boris and Clara to Neil.

Ask the same ordering relationship in several predeclared ways:

| Query | Expected answer |
|---|---|
| Which name occurs earlier in the sentence, Boris or Clara? | Boris |
| Which name occurs earlier in the sentence, Clara or Boris? | Boris |
| Which name occurs later in the sentence, Boris or Clara? | Clara |
| Which name occurs later in the sentence, Clara or Boris? | Clara |
| In the sentence, was Boris mentioned before Clara? | Yes |
| In the sentence, was Clara mentioned before Boris? | No |
| In the sentence, was Boris mentioned after Clara? | No |
| In the sentence, was Clara mentioned after Boris? | Yes |

Cross these eight queries with all eight exposed contexts: two lexical families, two coordination frames, two name orders. Proposed ceiling: 64 unique behavioral evaluations, plus a separately declared validation allowance. This is a new study, not spending the unused budget of the closed pilot. No hidden-state atlas is necessary for this diagnosis.

Freeze prompts, AI-only annotation and ambiguity notes, answer scoring, numerical checks, call cap, and decision rules before execution. Direct-name answers need their own tokenizer-aware scoring specification; do not treat a correct constrained-choice score as a correct unconstrained answer. Report full-vocabulary predictions and all mismatches. Use the same inference regime across formats and document that it measures next-token responses if no continuations are generated.

Do not repeatedly rewrite failed formats or select only successful questions. Report accuracy by format, truth polarity, candidate order, context and complete matched sets. The eight contexts are correlated, not 64 independent documents. This diagnostic does not support population estimates or novelty claims.

### Rival predictions

| Explanation | Observation that would support it | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Binary-answer or wording sensitivity | Direct-name answers track passage reversals, while binary answers remain constant or depend strongly on before/after wording | Supports task-dependent access; does not locate an internal bottleneck |
| Failure on the underlying order task | Direct-name answers also fail to track passage reversals consistently | Does not prove order information is absent from every activation |
| Candidate-position bias | Direct-name answers follow question candidate order more than passage order | Distinguishes one surface heuristic, not all copying programs |
| Reliable access to the order fact | Correct matched answers across reversed passages, inverted questions and candidate orders | Basic competence; no new mechanism demonstrated |

A stable dissociation must subsequently replicate on fresh names and verbs with all rules fixed. If no stable competence or dissociation survives, stop this direction before causal localization. Llama can later test replication; one model per family cannot establish a family effect.

## Main investigation, conditional on behavioral foundations

Use a narrowly defined role distinction as the first linguistic target. Cross relation changes with realizations that preserve the relation (such as active/passive), entity changes and question demand. Audit dependencies explicitly: the historical two-person role-by-voice factorial aliased identity and order, and must not be reused as an identified semantic contrast.

For each layer/site, construct a profile of intervention effects across multiple queries and formats. The profile must retain both answers that should change and answers that should remain invariant. Include same-answer/different-relation and same-relation/different-answer cases. Measure native causal effects within each model before comparing their profiles across depth. Compare timing and reliability separately; arbitrary depth warping must not manufacture agreement.

Distinguish sentence processing before the question, question-conditioned access, and final answer preparation. In a causal decoder, sentence states cannot depend on a later question. Probing can establish decodability under a specified probe; it cannot establish that the model uses that information.

Interventions require a development-only site-selection rule, a frozen rival prediction ledger, and fresh outcome-unobserved cases. Preserve identity, neighboring-token, sham, answer-identity and cross-document controls. Report every attempted site and failed transfer. Earlier failed period/component interventions remain failed; a new drawing of the curves is not grounds to repeat them.

### What would make the causal result informative?

- A position account predicts transfer following mention order when semantic roles and order disagree.
- A narrow answer-preparation account predicts effects tied to a query/answer realization, rather than consistent role-specific consequences across several queries and formats.
- A specified syntax-aware copying program predicts its declared marker, position and constituent rules. Construct cases where those predictions differ from the proposed relational account before inference.
- A relational abstraction predicts the full counterfactual answer pattern, including unaffected roles, across the particular realization changes being tested.

Multiple-query success alone does not defeat a strong syntax-aware copier: copying a rich context representation may preserve all those answers. An unrestricted copying explanation is not falsifiable here. If the explicit accounts remain observationally equivalent under the proposed interventions, do not run that mechanism test. Narrow the claim to measured task-dependent causal influence.

## Contribution and prior work

The possible contribution is a prospectively predictive account of how relation-specific influence develops and generalizes across linguistic realizations and output demands. It is not established by this proposal. A failure to generalize may support a narrower, reproducible boundary of access; a messy small pilot alone is not a paper.

- Burns et al., *Discovering Latent Knowledge in Language Models Without Supervision*: activation information versus stated answers already has substantial precedent. https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.03827
- Geiger et al., *Causal Abstractions of Neural Networks*: interchange interventions and alignment to interpretable causal variables are existing methods. https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.02997
- Dai et al. (2024), *Representational Analysis of Binding in Language Models*: low-rank binding representations and activation edits redirecting retrieval are already demonstrated. A new binding-edit example would not by itself establish novelty. https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.967/

Before making a publication claim, review full methods and subsequent work against the actual observed result. This proposal is not an exhaustive novelty audit.

## Resources and scope

The immediate diagnostic needs the existing local Qwen, saved tokenizer/runtime infrastructure, a behavioral runner, and AI-only annotation with explicit uncertainty. It needs no new model, paid API, training or large tensor capture. Later causal work needs a separate bounded protocol and authorization after design review. The existing instrumented captures can inform numerical implementation, but cannot replace new controlled outcomes.

Keep this experimental proposal separate from Joel and Armaggedon's broader linguistic theory and exploratory mathematics in Issue #35. Preserve all earlier negative controls and the original research branch history.
