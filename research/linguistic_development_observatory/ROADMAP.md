## Session 1 — read the black box rather than guess

1. Inspect original plots **and full-dimensional vectors**. Reproduce four site-by-depth contrast curves using comparable within-model measures, note source-pair clustering, and display individual pairs rather than hiding irregularities in the mean.
2. Parse exact token positions and causal mask implications. Ensure “same verb” and “final question token” have legitimate token alignment across manipulations. Inspect last-block scaling, relative depth and layerwise norms.
3. Identify *joint multi-site motifs*: rise/fall sequences, turning times, lead/lag between positions, inter-site correlations and within-layer component contributions if instrumented local runs are later approved.
4. Perform honest **existing-data-only** comparisons: role changes vs unrelated contrasts, controlled lexical-only changes if already recorded, wrong question and output effects. Report gaps, not interpolated empirical results.
5. Draft at least two incompatible explanations for the same resemblance, each with a potential observable discriminator.

Deliverable: first reproducible **observation report**, including what looked similar, where it broke, and what remains unknowable from the current record.

## Session 2 — build ideas and small mathematical toys

- Explore full-path versus cross-site and inter-perturbation similarities.
- Generate toy computational systems with known dependencies and inspect whether candidate descriptors recognize functional structure or simply generic schedules.
- Compare simple scaling/lag/Gram summaries with more sophisticated math only as needed.
- Keep a record of **compositional ideas** combining older methods in a new useful way.
- Request targeted literature audit only when a precise new claim exists.

Deliverable: honest “what measurements appear promising or misleading?” note and a short list of high-information controls.

## Session 3 — prospective linguistic pilot, if warranted

Design a *small*, genuinely discriminating fresh linguistic panel (e.g. crossing semantic role, active/passive voice and answer versus first-mentioned identity) and prespecify output controls. Source/verb/name family disjoint holdouts, explicit semantic gold and tokenizer audit required. Demonstrate model competence on relevant conditions; mistakes may still be used for descriptive work but not silently treated as correct semantics.

No paid calls/downloads or substantial fresh inference without the usual approval route. Do not reshape the protocol after looking at its supposed holdout. Hand the strongest model-specific claim to Codex for adversarial causal testing, without allowing it to replace our broad exploratory purpose.

## Milestone logic, not a forced publication timeline

- **M0:** reproduce and understand observational rhythm + controls.
- **M1:** distinguish generic-depth and output-only explanations, at least in a limited setting.
- **M2:** identify a nontrivial linguistic-conditioned developmental regularity OR document a rigorously reproducible absence under defined tests.
- **M3:** prospectively predict new linguistic behavior/causal effect; independently validate where warranted.
- **M4:** claim only the novelty actually demonstrated: foundational, compositional, empirical or methodological.

## Working practices

This stream can contain hypotheses that are wrong, half-formed and messy. Label them. Keep what fails. We do not need to decide in October whether the output will be a paper, tool, mathematical result or explanatory investigation.
