# Literature / nearby work — starting map, not a clearance certificate

The project values both **foundational discoveries** and **compositional contributions** in which familiar methods are assembled for a new validated capability. Prior art determines how claims should be stated; it does not forbid open-ended exploration.

## Representation geometry
- Kornblith et al., [Similarity of Neural Network Representations Revisited (CKA)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/kornblith19a.html), ICML 2019. Compare spaces; does not on its own identify algorithm identity.
- Geiger et al., [Causal Abstraction: A Theoretical Foundation for Mechanistic Interpretability](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v26/23-0058.html), JMLR 2025. Formal standard for causal correspondences; stronger than similarity.

## Temporal / dynamical trajectory comparison
- Ostrow et al., [Beyond Geometry: Comparing the Temporal Structure of Computation in Neural Circuits with Dynamical Similarity Analysis](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/6ac807c9b296964409b277369e55621a-Abstract-Conference.html), NeurIPS 2023.
- [InputDSA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25943), 2025/ICLR 2026: input-conditioned dynamical comparison; a close collision for perturbation-response claims.
- [Transformer trajectory geometry across layers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09287), 2026 preprint; path curvature, movement and layer trajectories already studied.
- [Path signatures / rough paths](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17634), prior mathematical machinery for order-sensitive trajectory descriptions.
- [Truth as a Trajectory](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.2073/), ACL 2026; displacement-based modeling of language-model internal trajectories.

## What is not a novelty claim
Cross-model similarity; seeing full layerwise trajectories; a within-path Gram or cosine; displaying attention maps; a task-conditioned probe; a residual patch that copies an answer; an R×V finite difference. A new **predictive explanatory combination** of these may still contribute, if it succeeds where strong published and cheap rivals fail.
