# Scope checked before this experiment

20 October2026. Targeted primary-source search, not an exhaustive novelty certification.

- [Aljaafari, Carvalho and Freitas, Findings ACL2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1964/), also [arXiv2511.20910v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.20910): role-conditioned next-noun continuation with contrasting scaffolds and role-discriminative filler lexicons, native dual-correct filtering, circuit attribution and training-time/cross-model comparisons. Within-role scaffold paraphrases are among their controls. Our question concerns identities of same-type people already in a passage, changes to one queried role while others stay fixed, and held-out-form prediction of source-intervention effects. This is a distinct assay, not a replication or refutation of their circuits, and no novelty follows from that distinction alone. The HF paper markdown/metadata endpoints returned errors; original arXiv HTML and the published ACL record supplied the methodological details. No author contact or dataset access is claimed.
- [Dai et al.,EMNLP2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.967/): binding-related low-rank subspaces and causal editing precede us. No learned binding subspace is proposed here.
- [Feng and Steinhardt,ICLR2024](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/9d1b7fc578c0d2d6431fc26d736ecaf3-Abstract-Conference.html): transferable binding-ID mechanisms already studied.
- [Mixing Mechanisms](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06182v2): lexical, positional and reflexive binding strategies and depth-related changes are close alternatives. A mixed or role-indexed profile is not automatically a new mechanism.
- [Language Models Use Lookbacks to Track Beliefs](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.14685v3): already compares source-only, question/answer-only and joint state donation; an observed handoff by itself is not novel.
- [Zhang and Nanda](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16042): assay/metric dependence of patching conclusions remains relevant.
- [Zhao et al.,2021](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/zhao21c.html), [Batch Calibration,2024](https://research.google/pubs/batch-calibration-rethinking-calibration-for-in-context-learning-and-prompt-engineering/): readout bias and external correction are established. Native-output predictors therefore remain serious competitors to a linguistic-role interpretation.

The possible new empirical result is narrow: a role-indexed predictor of this specific causal source-access response could generalize across changed names, question paraphrases and held-out constructions better than fixed generic, position and output-score rivals. It requires actual prospective success and an independent generalization study before a paper claim. One quantized checkpoint and one verb cannot establish a model-family law or a complete linguistic computational process.
