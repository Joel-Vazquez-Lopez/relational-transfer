# What the experiments actually established

**20 October 2026 — critical audit of the recoverable research history**

## My assessment

Your criticism is justified in an important respect: **the recent sequence produced much more experimental activity than progress toward the proposed linguistic mechanism.** Better instrumentation, another stopped competence screen, and an interesting output correction were repeatedly treated as reasons to keep the same broad narrative alive. They did not establish that narrative.

**We have not demonstrated a novel shared developmental mechanism for linguistic relationships across Qwen and Llama.** We have demonstrated narrower behavioral and causal effects. The strongest controlled training results are in the earlier synthetic systems; the strongest recent LLM result is answer-source steering, with its proposed role-specific explanation failing the latest primary comparison.

This audit does not run another model. It reports the positive findings, negative tests, invalid comparisons, untested proposals and technical failures separately. It also restores older findings that were being lost in the focus on the latest pilots.

## The findings with the strongest evidential footing

### 1. There are substantial causal results in the synthetic acquisition branch

The original D5 experiment relocated training support and found the predicted relocation effects across its24 models. Later fresh-graph auxiliary studies used72 models, and the rewire/repair confirmation used96 new models. Those are more substantial than another two-sentence pilot. They show that controlled training changes affect transfer and that ordered supervision can help in the particular scratch systems. See [D5](source_snapshots/outputs/d5_final_report.md), [S048/S054 in the ledger](EXPERIMENT_LEDGER.md#S048) and the historical source links there.

But the follow-ups matter equally: the strict12-node feature-isolation test failed, the same-distance common-neighbor rule failed across worlds, and the ability-matched auxiliary claim failed. The correct conclusion is **a causal effect of the manipulated training packages**, with the responsible general principle unresolved. We should have kept this distinction central.

### 2. The first trajectory metric failed a meaningful specificity test

For Qwen, role-reversal Gram similarity was about.82 while unrelated sentences were about.92; for Llama, .79 versus.90. Across-case speed correlations were about.98–.99. A metric that treats unrelated computations as more similar cannot support the intended semantic interpretation. This is an informative negative for that measurement, not proof that every meaningful cross-model process comparison is impossible. See [path controls](../causal_developmental_alignment_2026-10-17/PATH_STRUCTURE_ANALYSIS.json).

Subsequent studies did not repair this into a clean shared mechanism: output predictors and copying constructions remained serious alternatives; some comparisons stopped at competence; Llama's prefix check required a technical investigation. That artifact was real, but later numerical controls did **not** show it explained every scientific effect. Both statements must be retained.

### 3. Answer-source steering is real, but the preferred explanation has not won

The fixed natural-event patch produced9/10 target role answers and4/5 fully successful documents, with identity/neighbor controls intact. Cross-document donors produced recipient second-event answers8/10 without copying donor names. That is causal evidence, with a narrow interpretation: **the transferred state can redirect where the answer comes from**. The event-selector and order/address/copying explanations were not separated. The later decisive-witness study correctly stopped before running an indistinguishable test. See [fresh natural transfer](../maven_arg_feasibility_2026-10-16/FRESH_BINDING_REPORT.md) and [witness stop](../decisive_witness_gate_2026-10-17/REPORT.md).

The newest controlled Qwen experiment also produced real steering:117/144 source-consistent changed-role answers,69/144 complete hybrids, rising to59/64 on the native-competent subset. **The primary role-specific predictor nevertheless failed:** held-out MSE.570698 versus.504571 for the generic baseline. All-case and competence-filtered results are different estimates; the better subset cannot replace the failed primary prediction. See [latest report](../qwen_role_position_source_access_2026-10-20/REPORT.md).

### 4. Output correction and competence selection are a replicated diagnostic result

The prospective24-family test improved Qwen active accuracy55→89/96 but harmed passive95→87/96; Llama improved active69→91/96 and passive78→86/96. Qwen had19 positive,zero negative,five tied family effects under the stated test. A comparison of the same cut changed substantially depending on which competence filter admitted cases. See [prospective report](../competence_selection_confirmation_2026-10-19/REPORT.md).

This is useful for interpreting our measurements. It does **not** establish the mechanism you originally wanted, nor does statistical significance establish novelty. The post hoc assignment result is even narrower: an external decoder recovered36/36 cases, but deleting one Qwen role row still recovered36/36. Elimination could reproduce that apparent relational success.

## Where I should have changed course earlier

1. **Repeated competence failures should have triggered a programme decision.** Several atlas, component, source-access and coded-routing designs stopped before their intended causal test. A valid stop protects the evidence; repeatedly creating a new variant around the same bottleneck is not cumulative discovery.
2. **We needed a separating rival before another intervention.** Whole-state transfer, attractive geometry and successful output redirection repeatedly admitted copying, position, order or distributed output-preparation accounts. More examples of an ambiguous effect do not identify its mechanism.
3. **Technical repairs were too often adjacent to scientific optimism.** Padding defects affected goal-first v1–v3; initial name scoring failed for multi-token names; the first atlas had a serialization failure; batching/prefix shapes changed numerics; hardware interrupted Qwen attempts. Fixing these was necessary, but was not a discovery about meaning.
4. **Some comparisons were invalid in principle.** D6/D9 failed controls; D2/D7 initial probes failed validity; P2.2 compared unequal interaction expressivity; the scoring extension conflicted over person versus team. Their descriptive outcomes cannot be used as scientific negatives or rescues.
5. **Promising follow-ups often reused exposed data.** Development fits, reanalyses, adaptive diagnostics and fresh-seed checks are explicitly marked. They cannot be added together as if they were independent confirmations.
6. **I should have stated the gap to the research objective more plainly.** A useful evaluation diagnostic is a result. It does not by itself explain how linguistic meaning develops across a Transformer, and I should not have implied that another measurement repair put us close to that explanation.

## What this leaves us with

**Keep:** the controlled synthetic training effects; their failed graph-law controls; the bounded natural/constructed answer-source steering; the failure of generic trajectory similarity; the prospective selection result; the complete raw and methodological record.

**Withdraw as established claims:** a novel event selector; a shared Qwen/Llama linguistic development sequence; role-indexed access winning the tested alternatives; native relational computation inferred from external constrained decoding; a general strong-model factuality-composition deficit; a universal graph-neighborhood law.

**My preferred frontier, if we continue, is the acquisition question already supported by causal evidence:** which training comparisons make a relational distinction necessary, merely useful, or dispensable, and when does that distinction transfer? The existing history gives this question real positive controls, counterexamples and causal manipulations. It is closer to an answerable scientific question than another unrestricted similarity score. Generic hard-negative learning, optimization and architectural bias must remain explicit competitors; D54 and Track-B's bilinear counterexample already show why.

That is a recommendation from the audit, **not a novelty claim or authorization to start more inference**. A paper claim still needs a precise contribution that survives those alternatives and a fresh literature assessment. The audit alone cannot guarantee one.

**Current verdict: real bounded findings, substantial falsification, no demonstrated novel linguistic mechanism.** The recent programme needs a sharper causal question, not another promise that a positive result is one more pilot away.
