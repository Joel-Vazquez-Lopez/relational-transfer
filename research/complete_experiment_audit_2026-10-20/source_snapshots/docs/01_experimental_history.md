# D1–D9 experimental history

Status as archived on **19 September 2026**. “Valid” means the study met its own frozen gates; it does not mean every tested hypothesis succeeded. “Exploratory” means internally checked discovery evidence, not protected confirmation. Invalid results remain recorded but cannot support their preregistered causal claims. The contemporaneous [research log](../research_log.md) and original reports take priority over this summary.

Before D1, Pilot 0 and Task v2 exposed training, geometry, and candidate-construction difficulties. They are context, not D1–D9 evidence; see [Task-v2 report](../outputs/task_v2_behavioral_pilot_report.md) and [research log](../research_log.md).

## D5 as causal anchor; D8 as exploratory execution constraint

D5 is the main *acquisition* evidence because support topology was intervened upon prospectively with an isomorphic primary A/B relocation. Its conclusion is bounded to the synthetic oracle-channel system and to the measured final-query/behavioral outcomes. D8 asks more about *execution* in already-trained D5 discovery models: information can be locally present while its natural decision use differs; query and candidate pathways show different construction stages. D8 internal holdout strengthens the negative transfer finding but is **not** the sealed protected confirmation. D5 protected seeds 1704–1706 were not loaded for D8 and remain sealed. D8B/D8C were not run.

## Archive and reproducibility policy

The original `configs/`, `src/`, `results/`, `outputs/`, and `research_log.md` paths are retained; moving them would risk breaking code and report references. `.gitignore` excludes local PyTorch checkpoints, binary/raw datasets, large per-example/activation exports, ZIP bundles, virtual environments, caches, and credentials **from Git only**. These files have not been removed from disk. Lightweight reports, configs, source, audit/aggregate tables, run status, and pre-existing hash manifests are versioned. This Git repository is an **index and provenance archive, not a self-contained full replay package**. Reproducing checkpoint-dependent analyses requires the excluded local artifacts (or a separately governed artifact store), and the original reports specify their hashes. Protected D5 checkpoints must not be loaded merely to audit this repository.
