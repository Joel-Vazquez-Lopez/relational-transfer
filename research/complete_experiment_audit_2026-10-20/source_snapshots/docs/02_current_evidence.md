# Current evidence, separated by epistemic status

This ledger reflects the completed D1–D9 programme, not a Phase II result. The [chronological history](01_experimental_history.md) links frozen reports and hashes.

## Established / valid evidence

- **D1 (valid diagnostic):** the oracle-fed listener learned familiar relations nearly perfectly but failed the tested held-out H–H endpoint combinations while event information was largely preserved. This is a behavioral dissociation in the specified task, not a general claim about transformers.
- **D2-R and D3 (valid diagnostics):** the specific frozen simple probes did not pass their familiar explanatory/decoding gates. This narrows those probe classes; it does not prove that endpoint information or a nonlinear relational computation is absent.
- **D4 (valid exact replay):** archived D1 predictions and literal scores were reproduced. Final-query within-H endpoint contrast is selectively small, particularly for the H alternative present in the candidate set. Its preregistered all-corruption collapse criterion was not met.
- **D5 (valid causal intervention):** moving absent relational support by an isomorphic A/B relabeling moved final-query endpoint compression and endpoint-redirection failure across six paired seeds. This is the principal causal acquisition result.
- **D7-R (valid methodological diagnostic):** the D6 candidate design contains a candidate-only shortcut above four-way chance; grouped-held-out accuracy is 8,190/16,128. The original D7 remains invalid.
- **D8A/D8A.2 (internally valid exploratory discovery only):** endpoint identity is locally available; natural query and candidate decision pathways diverge at different stages; event effects remain comparatively healthy; tested simple transfers fail. Protected confirmation was not attempted.

## Supported interpretations, narrower than findings

- In the synthetic oracle-channel system, training support appears to control which endpoint distinctions are useful to the learned matching computation. D5 supports this causal statement; D8 supplies exploratory execution constraints.
- Mere local information availability is not equivalent to downstream decision usability. D8's token/CLS and query/candidate contrasts support this distinction descriptively; they do not uniquely identify an implementation.
- The tested fixed-vector/donor-transfer account is inadequate. This is evidence against **those interventions at their tested scale and interface**, not against every possible compact description.
- Shared-feature interpolation and context-specific distributed matching remain serious explanations. No completed valid study has quantitatively defeated them.

## Open questions

- What simplest predictive description, if any, compresses a trained model's finite decision-relevant responses to legal relational changes while predicting withheld response columns?
- What state boundary is sufficient? CLS alone may be non-closed because later updates depend on other tokens; all-token or augmented state may be needed.
- What aspects of training distribution determine whether any execution-level regularity is acquired? D5 identifies support sensitivity but not its general law.
- Do any compact primitives permit reuse and composition rather than interpolation or lookup? Can a nonlinguistic theory later predict controlled language behavior?

## Rejected or unsupported explanations, with scope

- **Broad endpoint identity erasure:** inconsistent with D4/D8's locally available identity and selective final-use pattern. This does not mean all identities are equally usable.
- **The tested simple probe-accessible factor code:** D2-R/D3 did not meet frozen explanatory gates. This does not exclude richer nonlinear/distributed access.
- **The tested simple fixed transferable endpoint vector / natural donor rescue:** D4 displacement inconsistency and D8 transfer failures do not support it. Other interfaces are untested.
- **A single clean D8 head or selective rescue mechanism:** broad collateral effects and poor donor specificity prevent this claim.

## Invalid / descriptive-only results

- **D2-INVALID:** object-slot prediction and fixed null-control failures. Its probe accuracies cannot settle candidate representational sufficiency.
- **D6-INVALID:** repeated-PAD neutral-message gate failed. Its apparently strong demand contrasts are not confirmatory; D7-R later identified a candidate-only structural shortcut.
- **D7-INVALID:** original candidate-scene decoder failed; separately named D7-R is the valid repair diagnostic.
- **D9-INVALID:** all 16 models failed the frozen ≥0.99 object-slot prediction-invariance gate. Its completed primary tables are descriptive only and **neither confirm nor refute** comparison-induced stabilization. Do not repair, loosen, or rerun D9 based on its outcomes.

There is no established computational grammar, language transfer, LLM generality, or unique mechanism. [Claims ledger](05_claims_and_evidence.md) gives allowed and forbidden wording.
