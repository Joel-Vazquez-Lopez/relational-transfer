# Research audit and the useful test

The original target is developmental computation, not a common geometric shape. Existing whole-state/write analyses fail to exclude generic schedules and answer preparation; period route interventions do not establish a transferable role message. The prospective competence-selection result is a real measurement finding, but does not identify a linguistic mechanism. Repeating those analyses or merely choosing another similarity score would not address the missing functional interpretation.

The three-role inventory supplies an operational contrast: one invariant role and two swapped roles under three possible future questions. A joint donor state must support all future demands coherently rather than redirect one answer. Cross-form source order makes role-table and mention-order predictions disjoint. Same-role/different-form donors and neighboring tokens test selectivity. Other-family donors diagnose lexical replacement/addressing. Original native failures remain recorded, so source information and native expression are not equated.

This continuation extends the unexecuted block-14 candidate to a **prospectively fixed depth profile at 7,14,21**. It is justified by the scientific interest in development and the inability of one arbitrary depth to describe a sequence. All three are committed before any causal outcome, and all will be reported. This is not an adaptive search for a favorable layer. No period or attention/MLP candidate is added. The new human instruction explicitly authorizes another independent implementation and necessary changes; the old resource stop is preserved intact.

## What could actually be learned

- A repeatable role-consistent transfer across all three questions, preserving invariant answers and syntax controls, would establish query-reusable functional source content at those sites on the conditional task support.
- A mention-consistent pattern would support surface-address steering under this intervention.
- Different patterns across the fixed depths would give a coarse causal developmental profile, not a universal sequence or a localization of first availability.
- Mixed or null outcomes constrain this chosen assay and will be retained. They do not show that relational information is absent from the model.

No possible positive outcome here distinguishes a syntax-aware role parser from a complete future-answer lookup table: both remain equivalent. Full-state bank replacement also carries multiple features and can produce inconsistent hybrid states. Neither mechanism novelty nor paper readiness follows automatically. A qualifying development pattern would require a separately frozen evaluation before generalization claims. No qualifying pattern means stop this inference direction after reporting every depth.

## Primary literature checked before execution

[Dai et al. (2024), Representational Analysis of Binding in Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.967/) already localize a low-rank binding-related subspace and causally edit entity–attribute bindings. Our proposal does not rediscover binding or claim that a whole-state replacement identifies such a subspace.

[Feng and Steinhardt (2024), How do Language Models Bind Entities in Context?](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/9d1b7fc578c0d2d6431fc26d736ecaf3-Abstract-Conference.html) already use causal experiments and transferable binding-ID vectors. Transferability alone is not novel.

[Gur-Arieh, Geva and Geiger, Mixing Mechanisms, revised May 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06182v2) already separate positional, lexical and reflexive binding retrieval, use groups with three entity roles, and study layerwise transitions. Neither three entities, competing retrieval accounts nor a developmental retrieval transition is a new broad idea. The potential narrower contribution would be rigorous coherent multi-question transfer under grammatical-role/order dissociation, plus a calibrated account of its limits; this experiment may fail to supply it.

[Zhang and Nanda, Towards Best Practices of Activation Patching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16042) already show that assay and metric choices can change conclusions. Therefore full native generations, graded scores, locality/identity controls and all dependent-unit outcomes are kept separate.

This audit selects the smallest feasible causal depth profile that addresses an unresolved function in our data. It does not establish an untouched research niche or guarantee a publishable positive result.
