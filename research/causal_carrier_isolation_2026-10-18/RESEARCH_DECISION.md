# Independent reassessment and decision

18 October 2026. Written before this pilot's outputs. This is the concrete response to Joel's request to reread, evaluate and choose a productive direction independently.

## Assessment

The original question is coherent: different Transformers may share some dependencies or relative processing order without sharing coordinates, amplitudes or exact layer numbers. Our evidence has not yet shown that correspondence on a linguistic mechanism. We should seek one reproducible operational dependency before inventing a new geometric equivalence notion.

We have spent too much of the recent sequence calibrating deliberately constructed mechanisms. Those controls are useful, but do not turn into an LLM finding by adding nuisance coordinates. In particular, the last reference comparison should have matched architecture before execution. Layer count classified its labels as well as the proposed classifier. Correcting that bank with padding or running another favorable constructed example would still not answer Joel's linguistic question.

## Preferred next step

Use the real pretrained models and a dependency question with distinguishable route predictions: **does useful sentence information already reside at a carrier token, or does the token keep refreshing it from earlier words?** A still-readable token can look like a completed summary while merely relaying repeated source reads. Cutting upstream reads to the carrier is therefore essential.

The protocol distinguishes frozen-source carrier access, live relay, complete sentence disconnection, adjacent-token access, and irrelevant-edge removal. All later question tokens are included to close relays. Source-isolated is not identical to a fixed vector, and is not proof of a semantic abstraction. The endpoint is a prospective answer-margin/accuracy signature on fresh lexical families. A failure stops the candidate rather than starting a search for another period, layer or template.

The old question/answer factorial was already done; it is reused here as a control, not presented as new. The actual new manipulation in this project is the paired live-versus-source-isolated carrier route, plus an exact cut0 dependency certificate. The whole-word answer/scorer defect is avoided with prevalidated single-token binary classes and unconstrained full-vocabulary argmax. This is a next-token task, not natural multi-sentence generation.

## Why this can help, and what it cannot do

If a model passes the cached signature prospectively, we have evidence that continued access to a source-isolated sentence token can aid multiple later questions across voice and fresh lexicons. If only the live signature passes, continued rereading is necessary for that tested relay. If closed succeeds too, earlier question states already suffice under this intervention. If none passes, this particular corridor/measurement is inadequate or the dependency is distributed. All are scientifically interpretable, but small descriptive differences alone are not paper-level laws.

Two independently passing model signatures would allow a limited comparison of dependency organization with model-specific timings. One model per family cannot establish a family law. Same answers alone, geometric lines, or fitted time warps cannot do this. Syntax-aware copying remains viable even after a cached signature: we deliberately stop claiming that this pilot could distinguish semantic essence from a sufficiently rich syntactic summary.

## Alternative directions preserved

1. **Descriptive linguistic atlas:** valid component maps and lexical/argument-grouping controls remain useful for the independent Observatory in Issue35. Avoid duplicating its full geometric exploration here.
2. **Methods benchmark:** pursue matched-architecture independently trained implementations only if the claim becomes a comparison method. Need genuine inferred transport, fair information access, appropriate sampler and full existing baselines. Current toy scores do not supply those.
3. **Behavioral failure investigation:** the reproducible mention-order/Goal format failures could support a separately scoped wording/readout study. That is not what this carrier pilot tests.
4. **Training-time sequences:** require comparable local checkpoint series and independently established task competence. They cannot be inferred from depth in one pretrained checkpoint.

## What publication would require after any positive pilot

A broader independently sampled linguistic set, prospectively held constructions, calibrated intervention specificity, attention-renormalization controls, and a supported comparison with existing causal-routing work. Select a claim from the actual positive pattern rather than drafting an impressive mechanism first. If this pilot fails, do not quietly switch to another candidate inside the same study. Preserve its failure, write the course investigation honestly, and make the next research decision from the accumulated evidence.

This is an attempt to make the research more resolvable. It is not a promise to manufacture Joel's desired result.
