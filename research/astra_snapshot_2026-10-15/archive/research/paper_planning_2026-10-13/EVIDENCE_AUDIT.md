# Evidence audit: relational transfer to paper claim

**13 October 2026. Audit, not a new experiment.** I read the experiment reports, protocols, analyses, and selected raw-output schemas across the controlled, Qwen, natural, and goal-decomposition branches. [Artifact inventory](ARTIFACT_INVENTORY.tsv) indexes 74 protocol/result directories and confirms their protocol JSON parses. Existing analyses report checkpoint and protocol hash checks where stated below. I did **not** independently rerun every training job, recalculate every historical checkpoint hash, or manually adjudicate every raw answer. Those are reproducibility tasks before submission. No sealed review set was opened and no new training was run.

The central distinction is between **controlled graph-world evidence**, **candidate-supported Qwen behavior**, and **open-span natural-language behavior**. Repeated prompts on one sentence, starts on one model family, and adaptive tests on already examined banks are not independent datasets.

## Integrity and claim triage

1. **Keep invalid runs invalid.** The scratch goal-first/v2/v3 pad-mask defect and Qwen repair-v2 missing-attention-mask defect exclude their numerical comparisons from scientific claims. Later corrected runs do not retroactively validate them.
2. **Honor adaptive chronology.** Several QA-SRL sets were frozen *after* earlier pilots but within the same source distribution; Gold 51, Gold 63, Bank 40/20 and EWT 75 have repeated or adaptive uses. Calling them “independent confirmation” without qualification would invite rejection. The typed fresh3 set was untouched before its token-control protocol, but the null control was designed after seeing fresh2.
3. **Separate label origin from label validation.** Original QA-SRL questions/spans are human annotations. Mapping these to actor/patient, selecting predicates, and excluding ambiguous verbs are not independently human-reviewed. The provisional AI CSV is not gold.
4. **Separate extraction, question classification, routing, and answer correctness.** Emitted `Actor`/`Patient` strings are outputs, not proof of internal representations. Deterministic routing is a system baseline given a role selector, not a learned internal mechanism. Exact agreement between `Answer` and a wrong field is not success.
5. **Honor the unit of analysis.** Graph family is the controlled replication unit; document/event or sentence is the natural-language resampling unit. Do not treat 5,760 prompt variants or two shared-item starts as independent discoveries.
6. **Preserve ceiling and floor effects.** Candidate-relative EWT scores can approach ceiling even with the sentence omitted; exact open-span scores can be near floor. Both conditions weaken apparent treatment comparisons.
7. **Keep the main distinction:** strongest current controlled science is graph rewiring plus ordered-role rescue; strongest current LLM lead is an answer-selection repair *conditional on supplied candidates*. A bridge claiming that the same internal mechanism transfers between them has not been demonstrated.
