# Literature map: post-training and subsequent learning

**Search cutoff:** 8 October 2026 (the date of this audit). “Through October 2026” cannot include papers released after today. Sources below are original paper or publisher pages; status is stated conservatively. This is a targeted, reproducible novelty audit, not a claim of exhaustive coverage. Searches covered `post-training plasticity`, `SFT RL future adaptation`, `SFT-to-RL handoff`, `meta-learning initialization`, `LLM loss of plasticity`, `RLVR capability pass@k`, `continual language model transfer`, and backward/forward citations of the closest studies. Claims in abstracts remain authors' claims until independently replicated.

## Synthesis and status discipline

- **Already established:** future adaptation can be optimized (L9–L11), deteriorate (L12–L14), and fail to track current score in SFT→RL (L1–L4). Training order and data composition matter (L23–L25). The proposed response operator is an organized collection of ordinary adaptation curves, not a new concept.
- **Conflicting evidence:** RLVR may mainly redistribute sampled solutions on some benchmarks (L19), yet reasoning-path or prolonged-training studies report broader gains (L20–L21). These are different operational definitions of “new capability”; neither licenses a universal claim.
- **Closest unclosed question:** after controlling starting competence and exposure, does *training history* predict **later learning from new examples** on independently generated task families under multiple update procedures, beyond baseline performance, diversity and validation loss? L1–L5 make the broad question non-novel; L33–L36 also cover immediate cross-domain and compositional generalization. Only a sharper causal and predictive test of subsequent *adaptation* may contribute.
- **Verification limit:** the 2026 preprints L4–L6 and 2025 preprint L20 are not treated as peer-reviewed. OpenReview access to L3's PDF was challenged; its title, authors and ICLR status were verified through the indexed primary PDF, while detailed results were not relied upon. No source dated after 8 October 2026 is included.
