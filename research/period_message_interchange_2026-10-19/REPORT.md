# A useful route need not transport the tested role assignment

## Outcome and scope

19 October 2026. **323/323 real Qwen7B forwards completed; 806 production controls passed; 1,136 independent saved-output checks passed.** No paid APIs, downloads, LLM training or technical failures. Frozen source/input/protocol commit `a2c71bd48f1544ae07f918a7f44e0670dc003fb8` was verified before any new model output. The prior studies and their failed claims remain unchanged.

**Primary decision: NO GENERALIZING PORT SIGNATURE.** No local account met all four development-unit criteria, so no positive prospective prediction was available. The fixed fresh panel was nevertheless completed as registered. Its profiles are descriptive; a favorable fresh pattern cannot retroactively become a qualifying development prediction. No statistically significant novel linguistic mechanism or paper-level novelty is claimed.

There is a concrete useful constraint: leaving a period route open can help the role×question contrast, while replacing that route's projected keys and/or values with those of the opposite role assignment barely transfers that contrast in the fresh panel. The earlier interpretation of route rescue as evidence for a reusable role-carrying period message is unsupported.

## Why this test was run

Earlier closed/source-isolated/live masks changed both access and attention competition, and left already computed suffix states in place. They could not tell whether the period supplied role-dependent content, addressing, or nonspecific modulation. This run used natural donor projected messages, separately at K and V ports, without changing the recipient graph for period comparisons. K and V are computational ports, not universal address/content ontologies.

The drafted neighbor intervention would have edited a blocked edge. This flaw was corrected **before outputs**: neighbor donors use a neighbor-open graph with its own baseline; the identity condition moved into the validation budget, preserving323. The original reviewed proposal, amended protocol and synthetic controller/tensor tests are retained. Mock323-call preflight is explicitly synthetic and is not included in the real 323-forward count.

## Technical validity

The actual hook passed independent SDPA/GQA tensor tests for K, V and joint replacements, identity, unchanged prefix queries and the distinction between blocked and open neighbor controls. Real-model original/wrapped native, original/wrapped F and identity controls passed. Every donation preserves the entire prefix through the period, both source-anchor trajectories and all pre-cut states; donor source messages are exactly invariant to later question orientation. All attempted calls were journaled before dispatch, and full vectors were saved before scoring.

The independent audit replays all 323 full-vocabulary scores, gold margins, calibrated labels, control comparisons, before-cut/source invariance, factorial contrasts, unit classifications and the exact development raw-file prefix hash stored before evaluation. All 1,136 checks passed. Prefix control arrays are represented as hashes rather than separately retained full arrays; selected traces and projected messages are retained.

V-only changes no Q/K at the edited operation given its current input; at the first edited block its attention weights equal the baseline. Later query states and attention weights can change as downstream consequences of earlier value edits. Therefore this is a value-port causal intervention, not a claim that all subsequent attention matrices were clamped to the unmodified baseline.

## Does a null transplant merely mean identical donors?

No: a post hoc donor-strength check finds nonzero role-dependent changes in every saved period port/layer. Fresh mean relative donor displacement across late blocks is roughly 6.7–8.5% for K and 13.6–20.1% for V. Same-role voice changes are larger (about 20.0–26.9% K and 24.8–54.5% V). These are descriptive norms, not matched causal doses; coordinate norms do not measure semantic content. The magnitude difference is a surviving rival for comparisons of role versus syntax sensitivity, and is documented rather than normalized away or followed by an adaptive dose search.

## What we learned and what remains open

**Supported local constraint:** the tested period-message changes do not behave like selective transport of the opposite role assignment through the late-block route. An accessible/useful route is insufficient evidence for such transport. Available recipient suffix information, redundant encodings, attention competition, generic boundary/cue functions, nonlinear message interpretation and donor/recipient inconsistency remain plausible.

**Not established:** where the binding is constructed; whether it is represented elsewhere; whether the period has no relation information; that all models use a suffix-only mechanism; a universal value/content or key/address division; absence of linguistic computation; a new mechanism. Keys can encode content and values can encode addresses. Editing all heads/blocks14..27 does not identify one head or a clean processing stage. A null could reflect redundancy or the wrong candidate site. The current binary task still makes role information interchangeable with correct answer plus question, so a syntax-aware copier or answer table remains compatible.

Dai et al.(2024, https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.967/) already analyze binding representations and causal retrieval edits. Mixing Mechanisms (https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06182v2) already separates multiple retrieval routes with counterfactuals and knockout, including pointer-versus-retrieved-content validation. Our useful contribution here is an audited constraint on our own interpretation, not a claim to have originated causal binding analysis or defeated those accounts.

## Preservation and next scientific decision

Complete323-call original-end vectors, three-anchor traces and late projected K/V arrays are preserved in verified lossless archive shards, with checksums and reconstruction instructions in ARRAY_ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.json. The final GitHub preservation receipt records which exact files were uploaded. Raw scores, errors, prefix hashes, complete individual/family ledgers, frozen sources, all controls, known-program tests and post hoc analyses are preserved separately. No weights, credentials or original supplied Gallistel manuscript enter the archive.

This bounded experiment is complete. Do not run more prompts until a positive p value appears. The logical next scientific question is whether a relation-specific variable can support selective transfer across independently varied events/roles and future query demands, beyond the current binary answer equivalence. This needs a new identification argument and a separate fixed design; the present null does not authorize chasing another layer. The Gallistel-inspired distinction between availability, causal use and behavioral expression remains useful, but the data have not validated a new linguistic processing stage.
