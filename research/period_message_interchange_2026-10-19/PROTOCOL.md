## Interpretation

K and V are computational ports, not universal address/content ontologies. A selective donation supports a local dependency, not a uniquely semantic variable. A null can reflect redundancy or an inappropriate site. Replacing all late heads/blocks does not locate one head or operation. A strong syntax-aware copier and cached answer table survive on this binary task. Native correct answering, calibrated classification, graded sensitivity and selective causal transfer are never conflated.
