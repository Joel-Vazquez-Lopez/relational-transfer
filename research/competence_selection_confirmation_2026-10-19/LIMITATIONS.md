# Interpretation limits that survive any positive primary result

- **The experimental replication is lexical, not architectural or linguistic breadth.** The24 newly authored families share one active template, one passive template, one question format and the exposed background. Freshness checks cover previous local INPUTS.json manifests, not every sentence ever processed elsewhere.
- **Construction is a bundle.** Active/passive alternation changes auxiliaries, word order, sequence length and token positions. A construction-dependent selection effect does not identify syntactic voice as the internal cause of the score shift. Absolute position, repetition or tokenization could contribute.
- **Directional arguments are not one thematic-role ontology.** See ANNOTATION_LIMITATIONS.md. In particular, recognition and startling are not uniformly volitional Agent/Patient relations. No human semantic or acceptability validation was performed, consistent with the AI-only annotation instruction.
- **Two notions of correctness.** Native is full-vocabulary first-token argmax, not full generated answers. The old fixed external threshold changes the classifier; success under it is not native correctness, semantic understanding or probability calibration. Zero-threshold alias aggregation, native max-token selection and off-label tokens are recorded separately.
- **Binary support is nonidentifying.** Role orientation equals a deterministic function of the question and gold answer on this panel. A syntax-aware copier or distributed output preparation can reproduce the contrast. Separability does not identify semantic binding or a hidden event-selection variable.
- **Model scope.** One existing4-bit checkpoint from each family, Qwen7B versus Llama3B, with different sizes/training/tokenizers. Fixed128 numerical controls address the diagnosed local shape issue; they do not establish full-precision or cross-backend equivalence. No claim about all Qwen, all Llama or stronger current models follows.
- **Statistical scope.** Only the Qwen family sign test is confirmatory. Its binomial calibration assumes exchangeable/independent family signs under the null; shared authored templates and a fixed lexical list do not provide a random population sample. No answer/layer pseudo-replication is used. A small p value, if observed, is not an effect size or a guarantee of novel/general linguistic computation.
- **Causal assay scope.** Only the already fixed cut14 is tested in this study. Source masking changes attention normalization and may recruit alternate routes. Equal neutral-edge counts do not match attention mass or positions. Preserved suffix states can already carry source information. Native full-depth traces are saved, but this study does not reconstruct all computational functions or provide within-block attention/MLP attribution.
- **Sample selection versus model change.** A gate changes the rows used in a report, not any weight, forward vector or intervention. Each selected mean has a valid conditional estimand; the inferential error would be silently generalizing it to a different population. Matched selected sets and whole-family gates are reported, and empty sets remain undefined.
- **Ratios.** Retention uses D_mask/D_native and is undefined for |D_native|<.5. Counts, raw D, negative native D, and medians are retained. Common gate support need not imply common valid-ratio support; inspect the separate ratio-unit lists. Any mismatch must be disclosed.
- **Prior work and novelty.** Jenner et al.(2024) already document structural shifts after filtering in an interpretability dataset; calibration and patching-metric sensitivity are established. This is a narrow fresh empirical test of a response-dependent construction-selection effect, not a new general principle. Earlier causal and probe failures remain intact.
- **Storage scope.** All full output vectors are retained. Native traces save all29 residual stages at period and answer; nonnative traces save five anchors and full-trace hashes. Full token-prefix arrays are represented by hashes, not recoverable values. No KV or within-block writes were recorded here. These omissions cannot be repaired by publication packaging.
