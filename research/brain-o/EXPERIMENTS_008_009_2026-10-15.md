# Brain-O experiments 008–009: Query routing and source attachment interventions

**Date:** 15 October 2026 · **Status:** exploratory, a single previously trained synthetic Transformer checkpoint. Not pretrained-LLM or mechanistic evidence.

Model: Brain-O 003 seed 1016, two-layer width-48 four-head causal Transformer. Two structured events and a question requesting one verb's agent/patient. 500 newly generated cases per overlap type in each intervention (2,500 per experiment). Training and evaluation share the same synthetic grammar and vocabulary.

## Experiment 008: intervene on query role versus queried verb

| Context overlap | Role-query change alters prediction | Verb-query change alters prediction |
|---|---:|---:|
| Disjoint | 100.0% | 1.0% |
| Shared agent | 100.0% | 0.6% |
| Shared patient | 100.0% | 0.6% |
| Cross-role A→P | 62.4% | 1.6% |
| Cross-role P→A | 60.8% | 0.8% |

On disjoint events, mean logit-vector L2 difference was 9.146 after role toggling versus 0.090 after queried-verb toggling. All comparisons are paired within generated inputs.

## Experiment 009: intervene on source-event verb assignments

Swap the verb tokens at positions 3 and 9 of the 15-token input; keep entities, voices, roles and the query unchanged. Fractions of changed model predictions:
- Disjoint: 2.2%
- Shared agent: 0.8%
- Shared patient: 1.4%
- Cross-role A→P: 1.6%
- Cross-role P→A: 4.2%

## Inference discipline

These tests demonstrate **behavioral insensitivity to verb/event identity** in a particular underperforming synthetic model. They cannot distinguish event-binding information being absent from information existing in hidden activations but unused at readout. Nor do they establish a novel learned mechanism. The model may simply be undertrained, locally optimized to role marginals, or capacity-limited.

## Proposed decisive discriminator

1. At the final pre-question prefix state, assess held-out decodability of verb-indexed role assignments using probes evaluated against role-marginal and source-position controls. Hold out combinations rather than solely random samples.
2. Check whether the information is causally used with matched in-distribution interventions along validated directions. Probe decodability is not sufficient for functional use.
3. Compare a matched trainable positive-control system with demonstrable event-specific routing. Interpret any performance gaps under matched compute, architecture size, and sampling.
4. Make externally validated natural-language examples an independent gate.

Runnable scripts, results JSON and a more detailed report are packaged in the accompanying Brain-O conversation archive `brain_o_008_009.zip`. No checkpoint or sealed evaluation was modified.
