## Executive assessment
Brain-O numbered experimental protocols 001–042 (including negative, calibration and incomplete protocols; not 42 independent novel empirical discoveries). The strongest controlled finding concerns a learned task-effective source-verb to query-verb to answer causal route in two trained synthetic 3-layer Transformers, along with separable participant-name to answer influences. A two-layer control learned 100% event-index classification at the verb token while failing to use it consistently for answering. These findings do not establish a unique semantic circuit, reuse, authentic-language transfer, universal depth requirement, or publication-level novelty.

## Essential retained corrections and negative results
- E031 falsified the hypothesis that the query-verb token immediately after layer 1 carries an already usable complete context-dependent event address.
- E037 corrected wrong fixed name-token masks in the mixed active/passive grammar used by E035/E036. Correct per-example layer-1 name patches gave 200/200 and 197/200 donor restoration; prior 148/200 and 145/200 were not cleanly name-specific. Source note prominently amended.
- E038: crossed event-key and participant-field activation interventions compose approximately correctly, 200/200 and 197/200 fourth-gold-answer matches in two saved 3L models. This remains functional synthetic factorization, not uniqueness.
- E039: a trained, explicitly structured lexical-key to record to field copier ALSO passed all corresponding output-level crossed A/B tests, 400/400 per seed in two seeds. This falsifies the claim that E038's crossed signature alone identifies a novel semantic-relational algorithm, but not the observed 3L causal route. Comparators differ in architecture and intervention interfaces.
- E040/E042: three-event colliding verb and tag keys defeat simple verb-only/tag-only choices (each 66.7% on generated audit); an explicitly structured learned compound-key copier solves held-out generated queries including 400/400 for an unseen verb×tag pairing in each of two seeds. No real language or unrestricted-Transformer generalization is implied.
- E041 plain Transformer training did not reach its planned endpoint due to execution limit. DO NOT cite as an unsuccessful completed run.
