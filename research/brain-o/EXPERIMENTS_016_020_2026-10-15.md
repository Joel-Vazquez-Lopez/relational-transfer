# Brain-O: Two-key relational retrieval (Experiments 016–020)
**15 October 2026 · Synthetic, exploratory, not a pretrained-LLM mechanism or ACL contribution.**

## Question
Do the small Transformer failures seen in experiments 003–015 correspond functionally to neglecting either event addressing (queried verb) or role addressing (agent/patient), rather than an unsolved target task?

## E016 — Learned positive control
A **12,964-parameter** model, explicitly architected with two event slots, fixed verb index, entity-only position masking, and two **learned** components (bilinear verb-to-event addressing and within-event voice/role-conditioned entity pointer) was trained on **answer labels alone** from the **same exact generator** as the historical **40,474-parameter** unrestricted two-layer Transformer.

Two separate seeds (1016 and 1017), **150 steps × 128 generated examples**, produced **3000/3000 held-out generated answers correct per seed** across all five entity-overlap kinds. Exact training-query duplicates in test: **36/3000** and **24/3000**, respectively; the rest were correct too. Same vocabulary and grammar on both sides; synthetic compositional coverage is NOT authentic language OOD transfer. Exposure and parameter count differ markedly from baseline.

The trained model has exposed event/role gates by construction. Patching either gate to reverse its choice reproduced the corresponding explicit query swap on all evaluated examples. This calibrates the interventions; it does NOT discover natural Transformer circuits.

## E017 — Sufficient causal explanation of the 50/75 shortcut signature
Setting the *event* gate to uniform, leaving learned role gate intact, gave mean gold-answer probability (both seeds): **0.50 disjoint**, **~0.75 shared-agent or shared-patient**, **0.50 cross-role**. Setting role gate uniform gave ~0.50 across kinds; setting both to uniform gave **0.25 disjoint**. With intact gates gold-answer probability was essentially 1.00. Use gold probability, not tie-sensitive argmax, as primary metric.

Thus, *event-address uncertainty alone is sufficient to produce* the 50/75 behavioral profile of earlier unrestricted models, but sufficiency is NOT identification of the actual causal defect in those unrestricted models.

## E019–020 — A four-corner query interaction diagnostic
For any fixed context x, define:
`D(x)=p(.|v1,agent,x)-p(.|v1,patient,x)-p(.|v2,agent,x)+p(.|v2,patient,x)`.

This is a standard discrete mixed difference (factorial interaction), not original mathematics. It vanishes if the output function entirely ignores either queried factor, independently of its answer accuracy. On 400 **disjoint** generated cases:
- Historical original Transformer seed-1016, 500-step checkpoint: **mean ||D||_1 = 0.02158**.
- Factorized learned positive control: **3.99998**; gold target has norm **4**.
- Either factor gate disabled: **~0**.
- Exploratory unrestricted auxiliary-head model at only 150 steps: **0.03054**.

On a separately generated 500-disjoint-case panel, the *signed gold-aligned interaction* `<D_model,D_gold>/||D_gold||²` was **~0.000139 original**, **~0.999996 positive control**, **~0 factor-disabled**. Signed alignment checks orientation of the interaction; raw interaction alone could be wrong. On shared-role cases the gold interaction norm is 2 instead of 4, so compare with proper normalization.

**Identifiability warning:** A direct parser, lookup system or query-time recomputation model could also pass this behavioral interaction test. It diagnoses joint output dependence, not cache timing or a causal neural circuit. Compass's matched-mechanism findings still apply.

## E018 — Auxiliary-supervision pilot (incomplete evidence)
An unrestricted model with small final-state event-index and role-index auxiliary heads (40,670 params) was trained in one *completed* 150-step pilot. On separate generated data the final head recognized the queried role at 100%, but event-index performance stayed ~49–54%, disjoint answer accuracy 48%, and mixed interaction norm ~0.03. Attempts to execute longer training were interrupted before full evaluation: **do NOT call auxiliary supervision a failed 500-step intervention or compare it fairly with the full historical baseline**.

## Scientific consequence
The past shortcut behavior can be given a precise two-gate functional null and a simple counterfactual-difference diagnostic. The hard next experiment is in an **otherwise matched unrestricted architecture** with naturally available event and role pathways, demonstrating by selective causal edits whether it learns and uses their conjunction. The positive control uses explicit structural scaffolding and therefore does not demonstrate natural emergence, a unique internal algorithm, or real-language transfer.
