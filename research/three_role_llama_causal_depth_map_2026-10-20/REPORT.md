## An important failure mode: name-fragment interference

There are29 outputs that are either unterminated or not a complete known recipient/donor name:26 terminated unexpected strings and3 truncated responses. They include `Agelia`, `Ag Basil`, `Delia` and an unfinished explanation beginning `Agorian`. No such response is discarded or repaired.

A post hoc tokenizer audit finds16 of these outputs exactly match a recipient-name prefix(excluding its last subtoken) concatenated with a donor-name final subtoken;11 are aligned to the replaced surface mention. Two further unterminated responses begin with such a splice. All29 cases and every matching decomposition are in TOKEN_FRAGMENT_AUDIT.json. Examples include `Ag Esther` from recipient `Agnes` prefix ` Ag` plus donor token ` Esther`,and `Delia` from ` D` plus donor `elia`.

This is evidence compatible with lexical-fragment interference,not proof of a literal character-splicing circuit. Last-subtoken replacement leaves other name subtokens and source positions intact,creating a hybrid computational state. Full-state patches also carry syntax,identity and position together. These failures materially limit a semantic interpretation of either positive individual answers or the negative coherent-transfer gate. The controls exposed the problem;no whole-span repair,new name set or additional depth is tried after outcomes.

## Interpretation and prior work

The useful result is a separation between recoverable assignment preferences,native expression,and coherent intervention transfer on this fixed panel. This experiment does not establish a novel binding subspace,an event selector,a universal layer sequence or a common cross-model computation.

[Dai et al.(2024)](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.967/) already demonstrate low-rank binding-related editing;whole-state participant-bank replacement here is not an equivalent localization. [Feng and Steinhardt(2024)](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/9d1b7fc578c0d2d6431fc26d736ecaf3-Abstract-Conference.html) already show transferable binding-ID vectors. [Mixing Mechanisms](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06182v2) already separates lexical,positional and reflexive retrieval and studies depth-dependent transitions,including three entity roles. Mixed outcomes do not falsify those richer accounts. [Zhang and Nanda](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16042) already establish assay/metric sensitivity. No method or phenomenon priority is claimed.
