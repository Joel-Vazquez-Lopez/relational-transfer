**Design-only review. No new Transformer inference or probe fitting.** The prior investigation was completed and preserved first: [results commit](https://github.com/Joel-Vazquez-Lopez/relational-transfer/commit/fbfb5066f34755ebad9b371117dc9ed5ba0f6ca5), [Issue35 update](https://github.com/Joel-Vazquez-Lopez/relational-transfer/issues/35#issuecomment-6256168571).

## Recommendation

Use the paper to separate five questions: what is recoverable, which graded preferences develop, what is causally used, what later computation remains necessary, and what reaches native behavior. These are overlapping functions to measure, not stages to assign to layers. Do not transfer its temporal-rate equations, event sample sizes, KL null distribution or animal learning laws to Transformer depth.

The strongest mathematical lesson is identifiability. On our two-person binary task the gold answer and question determine the role assignment; a richer similarity measure cannot distinguish those encodings. The older Atlas has useful same-question/same-answer contrasts with changing Theme/Goal membership, although only two independent lexical families. Metadata checks verify both statements; they are not measurements of hidden-state accessibility.

## Next step for review

First propose a zero-forward diagnostic: compare decoder accessibility with native scores and existing interventions, particularly unqueried Theme/Goal information under constant Agent answers in Atlas. This can reveal an availability/readout mismatch under specified decoders, not causal use or a new mechanism. Keep wrong answers and old failed gates.

If causal inference is later approved, the preferred small new test separates **period values carrying relationship-dependent information**, **period keys changing addressing/competition**, and **suffix-maintained response with nonspecific period effects**. Same recipient graph; K-only, V-only and joint natural-donor message replacements; role-preserving syntax, neighbor and identity controls. Proposed one-checkpoint cap 323 forwards, not executed or fully preregistered. The full instrumentation contract must pass before scientific outputs.

A two-event independently varied binding task is a stronger subsequent alternative for separating queried answers from other relations. It is not bundled into the immediate proposal. Neither direction establishes novelty over binding edits, causal abstractions, probing or retrieval-route research merely by being proposed.
