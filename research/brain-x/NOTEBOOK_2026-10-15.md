## H1 — Constraint-induced relational repair (current priority)

**Surprising insight:** apparently restored role understanding in a joint query may arise from inference over **multiple imperfectly encoded constraints**, rather than retrieval of an already resolved actor/patient binding. The answer format changes the factor graph executed at inference time. At its simplest, two roles are represented as an unordered pair plus a noisy orientation variable; complementary joint answers can be accurate without two independently reliable role decoders.

**Mathematical model:** binary orientations b_i ∈ {−1,+1}, local log-likelihoods h_i, measured parity/consistency s_ij ∈ {−1,+1}, coupling w_ij. Decoder: b_hat = argmax_b [sum_i h_i b_i + lambda sum_(i,j) w_ij s_ij b_i b_j]. For direct QA, only independent h_i terms may be effectively used; for joint extraction, globally coupled terms become computationally available. More generally compare marginal Bayes prediction P(b_q|local, relations) to P(b_q|local). **This is an established Ising/factor-graph operation, NOT a newly invented optimization algorithm.**

**Separating predictions:** (1) independently informative, genuine parity/relational evidence helps at fixed local evidence, output format, and token budget; randomized or contradictory parity evidence does not help; (2) effect depends on constraint reliability and graph connectivity, beyond two-name complement forcing; (3) reversing a single relation should yield structured nonlocal errors only if inference uses it; (4) conditional inference interventions differ from unrelated copying, comparable modular tasks, one-bit orientation rules and sufficiently expressive direct-QA baselines.

**Cheapest decisive falsification:** with frozen same local examples and query, cross valid, random, inconsistent and adversarially corrupted edge constraints, fixed token count; calibrate with exact Bayes, a shallow surface/position router, matched Transformer widths/layers/updates and a decoder constrained to output complementary participants. Predeclare improvement and causal edge-edit selectivity on heldout graph families. Failure of competent neural systems to exceed local baselines or equal gains from shuffled edges rejects this proposed mechanism for those systems.

**Literature/novelty boundary:** graphical-model/Ising inference and error-correcting parity checks are classical. Wang et al., ICLR 2023 (self-consistency, https://research.google/pubs/self-consistency-improves-chain-of-thought-reasoning-in-language-models/) already uses answer agreement; Geiger et al., JMLR 2025 (causal abstraction, https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v26/23-0058.html) provides intervention language; Wu et al., ICML 2025 (variable binding in symbolic programs, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/wu25j.html) already traces learned binding. Novelty, if any, must be **causally demonstrated prompt-dependent constraint integration** with specificity controls, not the math or the idea of answer consistency.

**Major-paper gate:** independently replicated neural constraint-use transitions across unseen graph families and real human-validated language, with causal edit effects and compute-matched retrieval/format-only/shortcut baselines, and a quantitative theory predicting when consistency repairs versus destroys accuracy.

**Abandon if:** improvement disappears after matched direct prompting, can be explained by complement-forcing or response formatting, persists with random edges, or competent models fail to use the constraints despite adequate training and controlled capacity.

## H2 — Computational scheduling, not relational storage

**Insight:** Brain-O's three-layer route may be a *critical path* for conditional joins rather than a role-specific circuit: source lookup followed by query conditioning followed by answer selection. Its bottleneck may scale with dependency depth/branching, not syntactic complexity.

**Formalization:** define causal transport matrix K^ell_ij = E[ TV(P(Y|do(H_i^ell←H_i^ell(donor))), P(Y|base)) ], conditioning on matched competent base/donor examples. A critical-path coordinate tau is the earliest layer at which selected-query influence exceeds source influence under intact-manifold same-query swaps. A constrained routing DAG with k sequential dependent joins has minimum path length k; **not** a depth lower bound for unconstrained Transformers with global attention.

**Predictions:** tau increases with dependency depth at matched sequence length, information content, and example frequency, irrespective of surface word count; equivalent-depth nonsemantic copying/modular tasks may show similar transport; auxiliary supervision alters learnability but not the route's algebraic specificity. A specific model's errors can reflect scheduling even when all queried variables are decodable.

**Cheapest falsification:** matched 1/2/3-hop programs and matched copying/modular rules; same model sizes, data budgets and competence, source/query perturbations and boundary cuts; OOD withheld paths. If tau does not track matched dependent joins, or simply follows fixed token positions/lexeme changes, reject.

**Prior art/boundary:** Wu et al. 2025 already documents multi-layer dereferencing, Geiger et al. 2025 formalizes causal abstractions, induction-head/attention routing research is prior art. A paper requires a new quantitative, architecture-conditioned predictive law validated on unseen structures and authentic language, not another layerwise attention handoff. Abandon universal semantic-depth claims immediately (Compass 12 already provides counterexamples).

## H3 — Relational symmetry as a learned operator algebra

**Insight:** correctness may depend on whether a model learns the *algebra of transformations on relational worlds*, not a localized relational slot. A model can answer individual rewrites correctly while failing their unseen compositions; this tests generalization at the operation level.

**Formalization:** let G act on worlds x (renaming, clause reordering, role inversions, event substitutions), with induced answer action rho(g). Define equivariance defect D_g = P[f(gx) != rho(g)f(x)] on semantic-valid x. For noncommuting update operations a,b, compare predicted f(abx), f(bax) to the correct distinct outputs; for commuting operations, evaluate the four-corner error including composed heldout transformations. A commutator [a,b]=a^{-1}b^{-1}ab must not be called a nontrivial relation when a,b commute, and comparing identical surfaces is a vacuous test.

**Separating predictions:** training on generator operations a,b but holding out compositions yields a strong gap between memorized surface rules and genuinely compositional operator behavior; a heldout commutator gives a targeted out-of-distribution probe, with name/position/voice counterbalancing. However passing equivariance alone remains behavior, NOT a neural mechanism.

**Cheapest falsification:** finite toy worlds with role swap, predicate exchange, participant renaming and event order; train on only individual generators, hold out compositions and commutators, compare template parsers, data augmentation, group-equivariant models, and ordinary Transformers. Reject if compositions match simpler position rules, collapse OOD, or require labels leaking the final relation.

**Prior art/boundary:** Stander et al., ICML 2024 (group multiplication cosets, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/stander24a.html); Mondorf et al., 2024 (circuit compositions, https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.01434); Geiger et al. 2025. Group representations, equivariant models and intervention algebra are not novel. A paper needs a specific newly characterized operational representation in learned language models, decisive train/test group splits, and causal explanation. Abandon if no benefit beyond existing algebraic/shortcut baselines.

## BX-001 executed pilot: relational-syndrome decoding

**Question:** can a small unconstrained Transformer actually use valid pairwise constraints that improve the Bayes-optimal answer beyond a noisy local observation?

Task: five independent uniform hidden bits b_i, five local noisy readings (flip probability 0.35), and five noisy parity observations on fixed ring edges (flip probability 0.08); query one bit. Input: fixed 11-token sequence of numeric one-hot typed features (5 local + 5 edges + 1 query), not language. Output two logits for queried bit. Encoder: 2 layers, width 40, 4 heads, FFN 80, dropout 0, AdamW lr 0.002, weight decay 0.001, minibatch 128, 600 updates, 20,000 generated training / 5,000 generated test examples per condition. Seeds 17 and 29. For negative controls, edge parity readings independently randomized; **first pilot uses unpaired train/test worlds across valid and shuffled conditions**, a limitation that requires repair in the next experiment. Every measurement is a heldout generator draw from the same fixed ring grammar, not an independent event or language family. Weights are not saved. Local and exact-Bayes reference decoder uses known generator noise probabilities.

**Measured results at 600 steps (not rounded in raw JSON):**

| Seed | Condition | Neural test | Local baseline | Exact Bayes |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 17 | valid parity | 0.6456 | 0.6484 | 0.7224 |
| 17 | shuffled parity | 0.6286 | 0.6286 | 0.6286 |
| 29 | valid parity | 0.6500 | 0.6522 | 0.7202 |
| 29 | shuffled parity | 0.6530 | 0.6530 | 0.6530 |

**Result:** the two-layer neural model *does not learn to exploit the available relational information*; it is at or slightly below the local evidence baseline. The Bayes decoder shows an approx 6.8–7.4 percentage-point available gain. This is a negative result for H1 as an account of what this particular neural network learns under these conditions; it is not a refutation of Bayesian parity inference. No natural-language or pretrained Transformer conclusion follows. No inferential p-values: test draws share a generator family and models use only two seeds.

**Partial three-layer follow-up:** a 3-layer, seed-17 **valid-parity only** 1200-update execution emitted test=0.6618, local=0.6484, oracle=0.7224. No comparison is claimed from this partial sweep.
