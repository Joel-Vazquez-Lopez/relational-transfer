# Primary-source follow-up during frozen Qwen collection

20 October 2026, after scientific criteria were frozen. This note changes no inputs, measurements, gates, interventions or predictions. It is a targeted scope check, not a systematic review.

## Direct availability/output precedent

[Villuri, Shaik and Doboli, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.04582v1) explicitly compare hidden-state probes, native output margins, and actual answers. Their logical-verification experiments identify threshold offsets and output-format failures, include held-out logical structures and same-word role-swap foils, and show external calibration can recover useful discrimination. This directly precedes any broad claim here that useful relational evidence can coexist with wrong answers or that output scores are a necessary competitor to internal probes. Our three-question contrast and source/question state interventions test a narrower participant-assignment assay; a positive result would still need independent generalization and a specific added finding. We do not replicate or invalidate their dataset or causal claims.

## Direct developmental precedent

[Agarwal, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.17537v1) separates when relation-type versus entity-specific signals control generation using final-token transfer, competing donors and entity-token controls across four architectures. This precedes a general story about information existing at one position before controlling output elsewhere. Their factual/morphological transformations differ from assigning identities to sending-event roles; our P/Q interchange does not isolate relation-type versus entity information and cannot estimate the same onset. Three cuts cannot reconstruct all internal functions. A source/suffix crossover alone would not be a novel developmental mechanism.

## Consequence for our decision

The current pilot can support only the protocol's specific predictive statement if its controls and held-out losses pass. Wrong answers plus useful logit contrasts alone do not establish novelty. A role-indexed result also does not exclude a syntax-aware answer table or question-specific readout computation. These papers strengthen the need to keep output-based explanations in the final comparison; they are not a reason to change the frozen test mid-run.
