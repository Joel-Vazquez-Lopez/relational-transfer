# Limits that apply regardless of the outcome

This note interprets the frozen measurement; it changes no threshold or analysis.

- **Average query selectivity is weaker than unique role identification.** If the three source-change responses for a candidate contrast are Δ=[0,2,2], the changed-role statistic for the second query is 1 and its direct response is 2. It passes those numerical margins even though the third query is equally sensitive. The saved three-query vectors are needed to see this. No claim of independent sufficient information in every query follows.
- **The output contrast is not hidden knowledge.** It is directly recoverable from native logits. A positive R can coexist with wrong answers and can be produced by a syntax-aware retriever or answer table. It cannot by itself identify a new semantic representation or internal inference algorithm.
- **Source/suffix interchange is a regional sensitivity intervention.** The suffix states already contain source information, and mixed banks may be inconsistent. A crossover in S_R concerns causal control of this output contrast; it does not date the first construction of meaning or establish that later layers only read out earlier information.
- **The role predictor also indexes query demand.** Roles have different question strings. A win across the two wording sets would be useful transfer but would not isolate abstract semantic role identity from every possible feature of the question.
- **The nuisance predictor is one fixed rival.** Its ridge regularization and features are declared in advance, with a small training set. Beating it does not beat every output-based model, optimal regularization choice or nonlinear predictor. It rules out only the tested predictive account.
- **Eligibility defines the target population.** The direct-response and normalization guards select edges. All rejected native edges remain reported; a conditional result cannot be generalized to them. The contrast's bias invariance does not make its full eligibility rule bias invariant.
- **The sample is deliberately small and dependent.** Two name/question sets, one sending verb, three forms, shared bases and rotations are not 72 independent linguistic replications. There is one checkpoint per family in the native comparison. The Llama causal prerequisite failed and supplies no cross-model causal replication.
- **Low-memory equivalence has a specified scope.** Serial streaming matches a pre-exposed full-vocabulary reference and passes declared execution checks. Peak MLX allocation is not total resident RAM, file cache, virtual address space or disk usage. The failed batch equivalence check is retained, and batch execution is excluded.
