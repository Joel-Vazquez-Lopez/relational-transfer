# What survives the accumulated investigations

20 October 2026. This review was written during the frozen Qwen collection. It does not add an empirical test. The current experiment's outcome is reported separately in REPORT.md.

## Strongest completed evidence

The [prospective 24-family study](../competence_selection_confirmation_2026-10-19/REPORT.md) supplies the clearest replicated observation: the chosen answer rule changes construction admission, which changes the target population for a cross-checkpoint intervention comparison. Qwen active complete units change from 3/24 under native first-token argmax to 19/24 under a previously fixed external threshold; passive units change from 23/24 to 18/24. Calibration both rescues and harms. The frozen primary family sign test passed, conditional on its stated exchangeability assumptions and authored panel.

For one unchanged cut14 period-source assay, the mean Llama-minus-Qwen retention difference changes from 0.0718 on the whole valid panel to 0.5860 on each model's native-complete subset; common native selection gives 0.5848. Under the fixed-threshold completeness filter, own-selected difference is 0.0401 and common-selected difference is −0.0703. These are different conditional estimands, not changes to the models or demonstrations that either checkpoint is linguistically superior. Ratios, sample counts, denominator exclusions and construction mix matter.

The general ideas of selection sensitivity, external calibration and representation-versus-use are already established. The defensible added content is this controlled linguistic case and its quantified inferential consequence. A paper would need to establish what it adds to that literature, rather than relabel calibration as a new mechanism.

## Findings that overturned attractive interpretations

- [Complete assignment audit](../matched_demand_handoff_2026-10-20/REPORT.md): Qwen's perfect 36/36 externally decoded assignments persist even after its sender-question row is removed. The decoder's one-to-one constraint can fill a missing answer. Perfect joint assignment does not establish independently sufficient evidence for every role.
- On those exposed scores, a correction learned from the other lexical set's surface positions improves Qwen independent answers from 86/108 to 103/108. This is an effective competing explanation, not a native repair or prospective confirmation.
- [Participant-bank causal study](../three_role_llama_causal_depth_map_2026-10-20/REPORT.md): no tested depth achieves complete bidirectional transfer in any of six units. Many unexpected strings are compatible with mixing name subtokens, limiting a semantic interpretation of whole-state patches at the last name subtoken.
- The matched-demand reformulation fails its native prerequisite. This does not identify a failed causal effect because that effect was never measured.
- [Current Llama screen](../role_position_source_access_2026-10-20/REPORT.md): 166/288 answers, 21/96 complete sources. It also stops before causal interventions. An exploratory graded contrast remains positive in 62/72 role-isolated changes. That divergence motivates a different estimand; it does not make the original gate pass.

## Why the current test is materially different

There are four participants and only three queried roles. Each paired source swaps exactly one role with the irrelevant fourth person, preserving the other two queried answers and the word multiset. Thus the changed role cannot be uniquely reconstructed from two unchanged answers by exhaustive three-person elimination.

The response statistic compares the changed-role query against the two unchanged-role queries, and then differences old/new sources. It cancels precisely stated shared biases. It remains an analyst's multi-query contrast, not an internal model quantity. Its native prerequisites, fixed sham, exact region controls and cross-fitted role/position/output comparisons define a testable but narrow question.

The scientific gain of a passing test would be predictive structure in this intervention response. A negative result would reject that particular role-profile prediction, not all linguistic processing or the possibility of a shared computational sequence. Neither outcome establishes an event selector, a universal model-family trajectory, or the layer where meaning is first constructed.

## A defensible manuscript direction

**Research question:** How do answer conventions and task eligibility change the linguistic computations that an interpretability experiment appears to explain?

A coherent manuscript could combine the prospective construction-selection result, the external-completion counterexample, a bias-aware role-isolated assay, and the current prospective causal prediction attempt. These are related measurement threats with explicit remedies and failure boundaries. This is a plausible research direction, not a claim that the present material is already sufficient for publication. The participant-identity assay should not be presented as a replication or refutation of existing binding-subspace or role-continuation circuit work.

The broader Observatory remains valuable as an objective. Its next contributions must be tied to identifiable functions and interventions, rather than similarity of plotted lines alone. The strongest current evidence concerns what our measurements warrant; a full computational account of linguistic meaning remains open.
