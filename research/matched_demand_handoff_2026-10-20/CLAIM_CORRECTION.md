# Interpretation correction to the earlier assignment-readout result

20 October 2026. The original measurements, frozen causal failures and reports remain unchanged.

**Still correct:** the external six-assignment decoder recovers36/36 assignments from each checkpoint's three-question score table under the known one-to-one constraint. Native complete-source correctness is15/36 for Qwen and29/36 for Llama on that panel. No internal assignment-solving mechanism was demonstrated.

**Additional limitation now demonstrated:** Qwen's36/36 survives removing the sender question's score row entirely. With three candidates for three distinct roles, the two remaining answers can force the third. Therefore the perfect score is insufficient evidence that all three queried relationships are separately available, or that every question contributes necessary information. This does not show that sender information is absent from the model.

**Simpler competitors now measured:** a source-position preference estimated from the other lexical triple recovers31/36 complete Qwen sources; source-column centering recovers34/36 Qwen and36/36 Llama sources. Both are external score transformations. The candidate-score improvements are not improved native generations, a new circuit or prospective confirmation.

**New panel illustrates the gap:** the incomplete observed native screen has only2/15 correct recipient answers, but its three-query composite role decoder recovers15/15 complete assignments. This is descriptive post hoc analysis and does not repair the failed native competence gate or authorize causal claims.

**Preferred future control:** four candidate people for three queried roles, with counterfactual pairs changing one target answer while the other two remain fixed. A dropped score row then cannot uniquely determine its answer by exhaustive elimination. Shared salience, lexical copying and syntax-aware parsing still require controls. The proposed96-response native panel is design-only.
