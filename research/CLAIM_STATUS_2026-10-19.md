This is an evidence ledger, not a new hypothesis or an experiment.19 October 2026.

|Claim|Status|Reason|
|The models can solve controlled binding tasks well|Keep, panel-specific|Earlier 95/96 panels remain valid; later Yes/No response biases concern different prompts/readouts.|
|Similar hidden-state trajectories identify similar computations|Unsupported|Generic schedules, output predictors and copying counterexamples survive.|

|Leaving the period route open demonstrates a reusable role-carrying message|Unsupported after direct test|Large earlier rescue can coexist with little/no role-directed K/V transfer.|

|The new local causal signature prospectively generalized|No|No complete development candidate; fresh profiles are descriptive.|
|There is no linguistic computation inside these models|Not implied|Our negative results concern specific measures, sites, tasks and claims.|
|A significant novel linguistic mechanism or paper is demonstrated|No|Neither inference count nor a selected favorable pattern establishes this.|
