# Alternative foundational questions

Scores are **judgment calls** (1 low, 5 high) as of 8 October 2026, not bibliometric measurements. “Novelty” means room for a distinct, sharply tested claim after [the literature audit](01_literature_map.md); “risk” means risk of an uninterpretable MSc outcome (5 high). Rankings favor a meaningful Phase 1 on modest compute.

## Recommendation

Direction 1 is the best MSc thesis because it has a clean negative outcome, responds to near-direct prior work, and can produce a rigorous result without inventing an optimizer. It must be presented as **causal characterization of subsequent learning**, not “discovering plasticity.” Direction 2 is worth pursuing only as a *later-adaptation* test: immediate compositional generalization already has close studies (L34–L36). Direction 5 is a cheap, useful fallback or control. Direction 3 is an intervention program only if a clear history-dependent data-value effect survives basic task-similarity explanations.

Speculative possibilities—models diagnosing their own missing capabilities or training directly on future learning progress—would need an independent task distribution, well-defined feedback and substantially more compute. They are not the first thesis experiment. A purported new method should beat early stopping, replay, PEAR-style preparation and ordinary meta-learning at equal total training/generation budget before being called foundational.
