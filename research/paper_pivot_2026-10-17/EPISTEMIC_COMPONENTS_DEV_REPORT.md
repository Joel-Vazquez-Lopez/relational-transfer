# Follow-up diagnosis on the same exposed development sentences

17 October 2026. After viewing the [six-pair joint-task pilot](EPISTEMIC_COMPETENCE_DEV_REPORT.md), I froze a separate [component protocol](EPISTEMIC_COMPONENTS_DEV_PROTOCOL.json) and asked local Qwen2.5-7B MLX 4-bit 24 single-sentence questions: one factuality-status question and one Agent question for each of the 12 source sentences. This is **post-pilot diagnosis**, not a confirmatory replication. The [script](epistemic_components_dev.py), [raw answers](EPISTEMIC_COMPONENTS_DEV_RAW.jsonl), and [machine-readable score](EPISTEMIC_COMPONENTS_DEV_REPORT.json) preserve the full run. No paid API, download, or activation intervention.

**Frozen scores:** status **10/12**, Agent **11/12**; three of six sentence pairs passed all four component questions. The joint two-sentence task on the same exposed pairs had scored 13/24 individual answers and 0/6 complete pairs. The component failures were `attempted to recapture` called NO rather than UNKNOWN, `could enter` called YES rather than UNKNOWN, and `Cvetković government` shortened to `Cvetković` under the strict full-span Agent score. The full raw strings remain available; no alias correction was applied after seeing them.

This pattern keeps a **composition/competition** account alive: the model often answers each sentence in isolation but is less reliable when asked to select between two described events with different factuality. It does **not** prove such a mechanism. The single-sentence prompts differ from the joint prompts, the sentences were development-exposed, and first-sentence salience fits the joint failures. The next scientifically useful test needs new source-reviewed pairs, component and joint prompts frozen together, an explicit first-sentence baseline, and a syntax-aware baseline. It must not re-score or tune these six pairs into confirmation.

**Current decision:** no activation patch and no paper claim from these data. The observed failure is a reason to study conditional composition on fresh material, not evidence that a reusable event-status gate has been found.
