# Paper investigation: does factuality gate event–participant retrieval?

17 October 2026. **Prospective design and source-only feasibility result. No new Qwen inference was run.**

## Claim to investigate

An event can be described as having happened, being possible or intended, or not having happened, while still naming people or things that would fill its participant roles. A model that answers “who did it?” may retrieve a named participant without respecting the event's factuality. The central question is whether factuality status and participant binding interact in the model's retrieval computation, rather than merely being separately decodable from the text.

The paper-worthy target is a **causal, out-of-sample interaction**: changing the status cue should affect an *actual-event* question while preserving the participant for a *described/attempted-event* question, and an independently localized participant intervention should change the participant without changing status. Such a result would support a functional factorization of status and participant information under the tested interventions. It would not uniquely identify an “event object” or rule out a sophisticated syntax-aware copier.

This target differs from the previous event-selector claim. It does not require four distinct Agent/Patient fillers in two same-type events. It still requires explicit local participant spans, unambiguous status cues, distractors that defeat trivial copying, and independently adjudicated answers.

## Exact proposed study

1. **Human source gate.** Select matched factual, negated, and possible/attempted mentions with explicit participant spans. Two annotators independently label the trigger, status scope, role filler, whether the event actually occurred, and any alternative reading. Exclude unresolved, nonlocal, or contradictory cases; report all exclusions and agreement. Start with a pilot of 40 candidate passages across several event types. The present automatic counts are *upper bounds*.
2. **Two question types per accepted passage.** Ask who was described as participating in event E, and who actually participated in E. For an explicit nonoccurrence, the correct actual-event answer is “no such event,” while the described-event answer can still name a participant. A possible/attempted event needs adjudication: “attempted” and “possible” are not automatically equivalent to nonoccurrence. Fix separate answer rubrics before model outputs.
3. **Distractor and transformation controls.** Include a second same-type event with a different participant where natural text permits. Cross order of factual and nonfactual events, source marker, participant name, voice, and question wording. Keep a valid witness where a nearest-name, first/last-event, and status-word-only heuristic give different answers. Transformations need fresh human semantic review; do not label a word swap as a natural source passage.
4. **Competence and baselines.** Screen the untreated local Qwen 7B on all required status and role questions before interventions, counting document failures. Compare to explicit extraction, nearest-participant, order, and syntax-aware copying baselines with the same text access. A powerful copier that combines status and role is a serious rival, not a straw man.
5. **Frozen intervention test.** Use development documents only to choose the model layer/site and any low-rank direction; freeze it before held-out documents. Cross status-source and participant-source interventions in a 2×2 design, with same-document, neighboring-token, sham, and cross-document controls. Freeze predictions for all four question/condition cells, including off-target effects. Report both per-answer and complete-document success, failures, unexpected strings, and confidence intervals by document. No post-hoc site or prompt changes.

**Predicted discriminations.** A status-only edit that changes “actually?” but preserves “described?” participant answers, plus a participant-only edit with the reverse selectivity, would support independent control of the two factors. A nonselective full-residual patch, status-word swapping without role recovery, or answers predicted equally by syntax-aware copying would not establish the factorization. A single downstream latent that encodes the whole answer can mimic output-level selectivity if edits are unconstrained, so intervention rank and off-target metrics are essential.

## Decision gates and realistic paper outcome

The next gate is **data validity**, not a GPU run. Before any held-out inference, obtain at least 30 source-valid, independently adjudicated multi-event passages across at least three event types, with a separately reserved held-out document set and at least 10 nonfactual contrasts. These thresholds are design choices, not observed feasibility. If this joined dataset cannot supply them, either collect a small new annotation set from licensed source text with a documented sampling frame or stop this track. Do not quietly lower the threshold after seeing model results.

The later gate is identifiability: predeclare at least one outcome pattern on which a factorized causal model and a competent copier with matched information and edit budget disagree. If no such witness exists, the paper must make a narrower **behavioral coupling / benchmark** claim, not an internal architectural claim. A negative result can be publishable only with strong source quality, a faithful baseline, and enough power to bound the effect; a tiny failed pilot is not enough.

**Current decision: promising question, no paper result yet.** The validation join proves that a real, public annotation substrate exists. It does not prove there are enough clean causal witnesses or that Qwen can answer them. The prior event-order finding remains valid but cannot be repackaged as a novel selector. No paid API, large model download, or new adaptive intervention was used in this audit.

## Subsequent source gate update, same day

The [36-card source review](FACTUALITY_SOURCE_REVIEW_2026-10-17.md) found **22 provisional single-event acceptances** by one nonblinded reviewer, but only **two** of those had a structurally eligible same-document CT+ distractor of the same type with a different local Agent. Thus the 30-natural-multi-event-document gate above has **not passed**; no held-out Qwen intervention is authorized by this design. A [reproducible cross-document pool census](count_fact_pair_pool.py) found at least one same-type CT+ source event in another validation document for each provisional card. A revised controlled corpus may concatenate **separately human-validated authentic sentences**, labeling the resulting passages as constructed, then test fully natural documents separately. This is a design change made before model outcomes, not a retroactive rescue of an intervention result. Its own minimum sample size, matching rules, prompt contract, and causal prediction witness must be frozen before inference.
