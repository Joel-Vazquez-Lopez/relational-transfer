# Open scientific questions — not established findings

This list is descriptive and does not mandate a preferred hypothesis.

- Does any model naturally compute a reusable event-role binding before a question, or reconstruct the needed relation in a question-conditioned path? Shared upstream information and coarse activation patching alone do not distinguish the cases.
- Are model-specific identity, reversal, actor-collapse, and patient-collapse outputs predictive on unfamiliar constructions, or consequences of template-based positional/wording heuristics?
- What changes during fine-tuning: relation-relevant information, its functional effect on answer selection, output specialization, or conventional overfitting? Historical objectives and adaptation strengths were not matched.
- Can a diagnostic distinguish known precomputed and reconstructive mechanisms in controlled reference systems without using a flexible alignment that manufactures the desired behavior?
- Will any reported effects survive independently reviewed *naturally occurring* language, unseen documents and question wordings, and strong format controls?
- Can a scientific explanation predict held-out errors and yield a selective causal intervention without impairing unrelated relations?

Successful answers on a fixed constructed benchmark, even at 100% accuracy, do not establish the underlying computation. Equally, failure under a particular prompt does not establish the absence of a relational capability.

No mechanism, intervention, or new ACL-level contribution is confirmed by this snapshot.
