**Critical measurement distinction:** output exactness, valid name format, candidate-scoring contrast, actor/patient correctness, and causally functional internal representation are different quantities. Correctly directed candidate contrasts do not guarantee correct answers.

**Known non-independence:** many prompted evaluations are transformations of only 12 constructed events / six name-pair groups, previously inspected in multiple research rounds. Their question-level sample sizes must not be treated as independent linguistic observations.

**Pending evidence:** the exact Llama per-condition tables, the latest Compass derivations, and Mira/Comput independent reports should be incorporated only after checking their raw files and implementation, without overwriting this baseline.
